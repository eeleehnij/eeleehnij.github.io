Arachnoid Membrane Segmentation in Intraoperative Microscopic MVD Surgery Scenes
Jinhee Lee, Hwanhee Lee, Jay J. Park, Jeong Woo Ahn, Jong Yun Kwon, Ciara McMahon, Julia Lewandowski, Seohee Park, Sanghoon Lee, Vivek P. Buch
MICCAI Workshop COLAS, 2025

Realistic Skin Trouble Simulation via Image Generation Models
Jinhee Lee, Chanhyuk Lee, Huisu Yoon, Semin Kim, Jongha Lee
IEEE ICIP, 2025[Oral]
[page] [pdf]
A generative pipeline for simulating the progression of skin disorders using diffusion and GAN models. A multi-paired dermatological dataset was constructed to capture severity transitions, enabling photorealistic and controllable progression synthesis.
Intraoperative Absolute Depth Estimation in MVD Surgery
Jinhee Lee, Hwanhee Lee, Jay J. Park, Ethan Htun, Bruce Changlong Xu, Sang Hoon Cho, Sanghoon Lee, Vivek P. Buch
IEEE CBMS, 2025
[page] [pdf]
Depth estimation during microvascular decompression (MVD) surgery uses DepthAnything-V2 to obtain absolute intraoperative distance measurements. We validated depth accuracy through optical calibration of surgical microscopy and 3D reconstruction, demonstrating precise geometric localization of compression sites.
