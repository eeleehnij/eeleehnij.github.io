<p style="color: #007acc;">
  Hi! I am Jinhee, currently seeking a Ph.D. position to explore research in computer vision, robotics, and biomedical AI for healthcare.
</p>

<p style="color:#333;">
  I have three years of hands-on experience at a healthcare startup, working on medical imaging and generative AI for dermatological analysis.
  I’m also collaborating on surgical AI research with SIMILab at Stanford Neurosurgery and neurosurgeons at Bongseng Memorial Hospital.
</p>

<p>
  I received my M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Ohio State University, and my first M.S. and B.S. in Aerospace Engineering from UST and Inha University, respectively.  
  My research focuses on <strong>biomedical image analysis</strong>, <strong>computer vision</strong>, <strong>vision-language foundation models</strong>,  <strong>surgical AI</strong>, and <strong>intelligent robotics</strong>, aiming to advance visual perception and contextual understanding in healthcare applications.
  Ultimately, I hope to bridge AI and clinical practice, developing intelligent and autonomous surgical systems that make surgery safer and more accessible worldwide.
</p>
