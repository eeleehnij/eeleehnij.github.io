About This Work

This project focused on the conceptual design and partial build of an Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) system for rapid supply delivery in disaster zones. The proposed UAV is designed to be rocket-launched, glide autonomously to remote locations, and deploy a parachute to safely deliver essential supplies such as food, water, and medical kits. Our team, composed of 9 members, carried out this capstone project, and I contributed specifically to the stability & control analysis, propulsion system design, 3D modeling with CATIA, as well as numerical analysis and code implementation. Building upon the design, we made a simple prototype using lightweight materials and an Arduino-based kit and conducted preliminary flight tests.

Design & Implementation

The UAV design combined multiple engineering domains, from specifications and structural analysis to propulsion, stability, and control systems.

Aircraft Specification

The UAV was designed to meet mission requirements with the following specifications:

Payload23.3 kg (food, water, medical kits)
Range35 km
Max Altitude2.7 km
Total Weight50 kg (with propellant)

Structure Analysis

We designed a 3D model using CATIA and performed structural stress and deformation analysis using FEA tools to ensure it could withstand launch and glide loads. The analysis validated the selected material thickness and safety factor for each major component.

Propulsion

The UAV used a solid-propellant rocket booster to achieve launch altitude. Grain design and nozzle optimization were carried out to meet thrust requirements.

  • Thrust: 773 N × 3 boosters
  • Burn time: 20 sec
  • Max altitude: 2,760 m

A rocket separation mechanism was designed to deploy wings and transition to glide flight after booster burnout.

Stability & Control

We analyzed longitudinal, lateral, and directional stability using DATCOM.
A PID-based control system was implemented to maintain stable flight during glide descent.

Prototype & Testing

Following the conceptual design and system analysis above, Our team fabricated a lightweight prototype incorporating an embedded control system and conducted preliminary flight tests to evaluate the overall airframe configuration and control algorithms.

  • Constructed a functional prototype based on the finalized UAV design
  • Implemented an Arduino-based embedded control system to evaluate flight stability and control algorithms
  • Conducted multiple preliminary flight tests to validate aerodynamic and control behavior
