About This Work

This project aimed to develop deep learning-based Personal Color Analysis models. I mentored a team of 3 undergraduate student interns throughout the entire process, guiding them to successfully complete the project. Personal color refers to an individual's most suitable color palette based on skin tone, hair, eye color, and other features, often used in fashion and beauty domains. Our system was designed to classify personal color type into 8 classes:
Spring (Light/Bright), Summer (Light/Mute), Fall (Mute/Dark), Winter (Bright/Deep).
The project covered data collection and annotation, model training and validation, as well as personalized cosmetic recommendation based on the predicted color type. We also developed a demo app using Android Studio, integrating the model to provide real-time inference from facial images.

Methodology

1. Data Collection & Annotation

We collected a curated dataset of facial images labeled with personal color types. Our dataset included diverse skin tones and lighting conditions, sourced from voth general photo collection and public dataset (FFHQ). Manual annotation was performed by referencing professional color consulting materials and validating the labels with personal color experts to ensure label accuracy and consistency.

2. Model Training

We trained CNN models (ResNet, EfficientNet) to classify personal color into 8 categories. We applied data augmentation excepting color augmentation to preserve color features. Our model achieved 88.8% accuracy in Warm/Cool tone classification, 81.6% accuracy in Seasonal type (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter), and 70.93% overall accuracy across 8 subtone categories.

3. Android App Development

We developed an Android demo app using Android Studio. Users could upload or capture a face image and receive their personal color type in real-time. Based on the predicted result, the app also recommend cosmetic products that match the user's most suitable color palette and the worst color palette.
