About This Work

This project focused on developing a chatbot application that integrates ChatGPT with skin analysis and skincare recommendation system. The system was designed to provide skincare consultations and product recommendations through natural conversation. The project was conducted in collaboration with undergraduate interns from Sungkyunkwan University, combining research and product development under industry–academia partnership.

Methodology

The chatbot was developed using the ChatGPT API and integrated with Lululab’s proprietary skin analysis, skin-type classification, and cosmetic recommendation systems. I designed and implemented both the Android chatbot application and the Python-based backend, building a complete conversational AI pipeline that enables multimodal interaction and product recommendation.

The project initially began as an industry–academia collaboration with three students from Sungkyunkwan University, where I supervised the development environment setup and guided early app prototyping. Beyond this stage, I led the internal development—refactoring the app, integrating API modules, and implementing function calling mechanisms to link ChatGPT with skin analysis and recommendation models. To enhance the chatbot’s domain reasoning, I redefined ChatGPT’s system roles for dermatology-related dialogue and applied >RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)> to allow the model to retrieve product and skincare knowledge dynamically.

Users can take a photo and send it to the chatbot, which then analyzes their skin condition and provides a report.

They can also request more detailed information from the chatbot, including analysis scores, skin segmentations, and recommended skincare products.
